Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

This policy explains what personal information edwardmartimbor.com collects, why, and what you can do about it. I follow the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and, for visitors in the European Union and the UK, the GDPR.

Who I am

This site is run by Edward J. Martimbor, based in the Philippines. I’m responsible for (the “controller” of) the information described here. To reach me about privacy, use the request form at the bottom of this page.

What I collect and why

  • Course waitlist: your email, your first name if you give it, and which of my books you own. I use it to send you one email when the course opens, with your reader code. Legal basis: your request and your consent. I don’t add you to a newsletter.
  • Course enrollment (when it opens): the course runs on a separate course platform with its own sign-up, and payments are handled by Stripe or PayPal. I never see or store your card number. I’ll name the course platform here before enrollment opens.
  • Privacy requests: the details you put in the request form, so I can answer you. Legal basis: my legal obligations.
  • Basic server logs: my host, Netlify, briefly records technical data such as IP addresses to keep the site running and secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a working, secure website.

This site doesn’t use cookies for tracking, advertising or analytics, and doesn’t load fonts, scripts or trackers from other companies. Links to Amazon take you to Amazon’s site, where Amazon’s own privacy policy applies.

Who else handles your information

Netlify, Inc. (USA) hosts this site and stores form submissions, so your information may be transferred outside the Philippines or the EU. I only use providers that protect personal data under contract, such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. I never sell, rent or trade your information.

How long I keep it

  • Waitlist entries: until the course-opening email has been sent and 12 months have passed, or sooner if you ask me to delete them.
  • Privacy requests: up to two years after I close the request.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information I hold about you, correct it, delete it, receive a copy, or object to or restrict how I use it. You can withdraw your consent at any time. I answer within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to the Philippine National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph) or, in the EU or UK, to your local data protection authority.

Children

This site is for adults. I don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If I change this policy, I’ll update the date at the top.
