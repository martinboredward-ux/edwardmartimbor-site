Chapter 1: Healthcare IT: When Technology Supports Patient Care

The big idea

An available system is not the same as a working clinical service. Patient care depends on workflows, not just uptime.

Technology supports patient care; it does not define it. A server that is "up" according to monitoring can still leave nurses unable to see lab results, or registration staff unable to check a patient in. The leadership skill this chapter builds is telling the difference — and knowing who is accountable for closing that gap.

You will build

  1. A map of one real clinical workflow, validated by the people who do the work.
  2. A list of that workflow's technology dependencies and its most consequential failure point.
  3. Clear ownership: who leads, who investigates, who communicates, who verifies restoration.
  4. Practice telling a technical-only ticket apart from a workflow-aware one.
  5. A four-week plan to put this into practice with your own team.
