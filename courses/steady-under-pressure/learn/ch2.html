Chapter 2: Understanding the Healthcare Technology Environment

The big idea

An asset inventory tells you what you own. A dependency map tells you what a service actually needs to keep working, end to end.

Most healthcare IT environments already have an asset list somewhere. Far fewer have a map of which five or six services, if interrupted, would actually stop patient-facing work — and what each of those services quietly depends on: identity, interfaces, network, vendor hosting, specialized devices.

You will build

  1. A short list of your five most critical services, ranked by consequence of interruption.
  2. One fully mapped service dependency chain, end to end.
  3. A compact dependency register, linked to your CMDB rather than duplicating it.
  4. A review of hidden failure points: legacy systems, single points of knowledge, expired certificates.
  5. A four-week plan so a new on-call technician isn't dependent on one person's memory.
