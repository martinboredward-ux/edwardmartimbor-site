Inside the workbook

Turn leadership lessons into everyday practice

  • Assess service desk practices, ownership, and clinical workflow impact.
  • Work through incidents, changes, recovery, and security decisions.
  • Clarify vendor responsibilities, governance, and meaningful measures.
  • Lead team workshops, tabletop exercises, and coaching conversations.
  • Build focused 30-day improvements and a final 90-day leadership plan.

Individual leader

Complete the assessment and document evidence. Choose one priority and own the 30-day plan. Use templates to draft local procedures.

Team facilitator

Invite frontline IT staff and clinical or operations partners. Run the tabletop and record a decision, owner, and date. Review and approve changes before rollout.

Large-format pages leave room to record decisions, assign owners, and return to the evidence after the work is done. Designed to be used alongside Steady Under Pressure.

Free sample

Try three Chapter 1 worksheets

Worksheet 1.1

Map the Patient Journey

Select one workflow and map its 5 to 10 actual steps, then validate the map with the people who perform the work.

Worksheet 1.2

Dependencies and Failure Points

List what each workflow step depends on, what happens if it fails, and who owns it. Check the hidden dependencies: identity, interfaces, network, printing, scanners, vendor-hosted services, wireless coverage, and manual handoffs.

Worksheet 1.3

Impact Before Priority

Capture the clinical and operational facts before escalating: what work cannot be completed, who is affected, whether care is delayed, and what is still unknown.
