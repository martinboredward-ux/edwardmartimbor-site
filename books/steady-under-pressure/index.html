A Practical Guide to Healthcare IT Service Management and Leadership

Lead healthcare IT with confidence, clarity, and resilience. A practical, real-world guide for healthcare IT leaders and service desk managers who keep care moving, systems reliable, and people supported, especially when the pressure is high.

What you’ll learn

Proven strategies and field-tested lessons

Drawing on more than 30 years of hands-on experience, the book delivers prescriptive guidance for building a high-performing, reliable, and patient-focused IT service operation.

  • Build a service desk that delivers consistent, high-quality support.
  • Lead, develop, and retain a strong IT support team.
  • Apply ITIL-inspired best practices without the complexity.
  • Align IT services with clinical and operational goals.
  • Prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions.
  • Use metrics and continuous improvement to drive results.

Whether you’re a new manager or a seasoned IT leader, Steady Under Pressure gives you a clear, actionable framework to lead with purpose, keep operations stable, and make sure technology supports what matters most: exceptional patient care.

Read Chapter 1

Healthcare IT: When Technology Supports Patient Care

A retail system failure can interrupt transactions. In healthcare, a technology failure can affect how information reaches the people making decisions about patient care. Chapter 1 shows how to tell an available system apart from a clinical workflow that staff can actually use.

A technology inventory tells you what the organization owns. A workflow map tells you what the organization depends on.

Leadership principle, Chapter 1

Inside the chapter: start with the clinical workflow · patient care begins at registration · define impact before assigning priority · treat communication as part of restoration · make ownership unambiguous · an illustrative scenario where the system is up but results are late · your first 30 days: a four-week action plan.
